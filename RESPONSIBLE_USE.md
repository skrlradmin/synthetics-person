# Responsible use
- **Fictional adults only.** Every character must be an invented adult (21+), and must clearly look adult.
- **No real-person likeness.** Do not train on, prompt for, or reference any real or identifiable person, celebrity or private individual. Don't use real photos as identity references.
- **Keep synthetic provenance.** Keep generation metadata (model, seed, prompt). Don't strip or forge provenance, and disclose AI generation wherever platforms or laws require it.
- **No deception or harm.** Don't use characters for impersonation, fraud, harassment or non-consensual content.
- **Respect model licenses.** Some base models are non-commercial only (see docs/licenses.md).
